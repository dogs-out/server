Delete your account

Dogs Out

Delete it yourself, in the app

This takes effect immediately and needs no request or waiting period.

  1. Open Dogs Out and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap the gear icon in the top right to open Settings.
  4. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  5. Tap Delete forever to confirm. Your account and data are erased straight away.

If you can't sign in

Email help@dogsout.app from the address you registered with and ask us to delete your account. We use that address only to confirm the account is yours, and we action the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Everything below is erased permanently. Deletion cannot be undone, and accounts cannot be restored.

  • Your account and login details, including your email address and password
  • Your profile: name, date of birth, bio, photos, interests and location
  • Your dogs, including their profiles and photos
  • Your matches and swipe history
  • Your chat messages, which are removed for both you and the people you spoke with
  • Playdates you created, and your participation in other people's playdates
  • Your blocks and reports
  • Your notification token, so notifications stop reaching your device

What we keep, and for how long

We keep no user data after deletion for our own purposes. There is no retention period on your profile, photos, messages or account.

Two technical exceptions exist. Deleted records can persist briefly in routine encrypted backups of our database until those backups age out and are overwritten; they are never used to bring an individual account back. Server logs may contain an email address for a short period as part of normal operation. Neither is used to identify or contact you after deletion.
