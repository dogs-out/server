Terms & Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who can use Dogs Out

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. You are responsible for the accuracy of your profile and for keeping your login credentials safe.

2. Your content & behaviour

Be kind. Content that is abusive, discriminatory, sexually explicit, misleading, or unrelated to meeting other dog owners or arranging dogsitting is not allowed. We filter obvious profanity automatically and review every user report. We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these rules. You can report or block any user from your chat with them.

3. Meeting in person

Dogs Out helps you meet other dog owners, but meetings happen at your own responsibility. Meet in public places like parks, and make sure your dog is comfortable around other dogs.

4. Dogsitting

Dogs Out only provides a way for owners and sitters to find and contact each other. We are not a party to any sitting arrangement: we do not employ, vet, endorse, or supervise sitters, and we do not verify any user's experience, qualifications, or other self-declared information. Whether, and on what terms, you arrange dogsitting is decided entirely between you and the other user. We strongly recommend a personal interview and a trial meet-and-greet with the dog first, and agreeing on all details (times, care instructions, compensation, emergencies) in advance. Owners remain responsible for their dogs, and sitters for the care they agree to provide. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Dogs Out is not liable for any injury, loss, or damage arising from dogsitting arrangements made through the app.

5. What data we store

Your account (name, email, date of birth), your profile and dog profiles including photos, your matches and chat messages, your notification preferences, and a notification token identifying your device so we can deliver push notifications.

Location. We store your precise location as coordinates, which you set from your device or update at any time in the app. We use it only to work out how far away other users are and to find parks near you when you create a playdate. Other users see how far away you are, never your coordinates or address.

Photos. Photos you upload are stored as image files on our hosting provider's storage and delivered to the app over a link. Anyone who has that link can open the photo without signing in, so treat a photo you upload as visible to the people who can see your profile. We remove image metadata, including any GPS coordinates the camera recorded, before storing it. Deleting a photo or your account deletes the underlying files.

6. How your data is used

Your data is used solely to run Dogs Out: showing your profile to nearby users, delivering messages, and sending push notifications you can switch off at any time. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Reported chats are reviewed by our moderation team.

A few companies process data on our behalf so the app can work: our hosting provider stores the database and your photos, an email provider sends verification and password-reset emails, a notification service delivers push notifications to your device, and Google provides sign-in, maps, and park search. They may only use what we send them to provide that service to us.

7. Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account permanently removes your profile, photos, dogs, matches, chat messages, playdates, blocks, and your notification token. Your messages are removed for the people you spoke with as well. This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Deleted records can remain briefly in routine encrypted backups until those age out and are overwritten, and an email address may appear in server logs for a short time; neither is used to identify or contact you afterwards. Full steps, including what to do if you can no longer sign in, are on the Delete your account page.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms or your data? Contact us at help@dogsout.app.
