Account & data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. See Delete your account for the exact steps and what is removed, or our Privacy Policy for details on what data we store and how it's used.

Reporting a user

You can report or block another user directly from your chat with them. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team. See our Child Safety Standards for how we handle reports involving children.
