Feedback

Ideas, bugs, things that confused you, features you'd love — we read every message, and a lot of what's in the app today started as someone's feedback.

In the app

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Choose Send Feedback, write your message and send it.

This is the quickest way: it tells us which app version you're on, so a bug is easier to track down.

By email

Can't open the app, or want to attach a screenshot? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Email help@dogsout.app

Something unsafe?

To report a person or a message, use Report in the ⋯ menu on their card or in the chat, so it reaches our moderation team straight away. If someone is in danger, call the police (117 in Switzerland) first.
